3D Marine High Voltage – Step-by-Step Tutorial Guide

Mission 1: Bow Thruster 6.6 kV Motor Isolation & PTW
Follow each step below. Click "▶ Do This Step" or operate the 3D Switchboard controls directly.
UK MCA COSWP Chapter 22 & STCW 2010 Manila Amendments Table A-III/6:
Any work on marine high voltage (> 1,000 V) equipment must strictly follow the 5 Golden Rules in chronological order.
1. Disconnect Completely (All-Pole Isolation)

Isolate the apparatus from all sources of electrical supply by tripping the Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) and racking the carriage out to DISCONNECTED (0/20), including secondary 440 V back-feed breakers.

2. Secure Against Re-Connection (LOTO & Castell Keys)

Padlock the VCB racking shutter port, extract Castell trapped keys, transfer them to the Earth Switch, lock keys in the Safety Key Box, and post Danger warning tags.

3. Verify That the Installation is Dead (Prove – Test – Prove)

Test all three phases (L1, L2, L3) using a rated 6.6 kV live-line voltage detector wand proven on a proving unit immediately before and immediately after testing.

4. Carry Out Earthing and Short-Circuiting

Close the Integral Fault-Making Earth Switch (EK6) and/or connect Portable Circuit Main Earth (CME) leads (PE ship hull bar FIRST, then L1, L2, L3).

5. Protect Against Adjacent Live Parts & Issue Permit (PTW)

Padlock automatic red safety shutters covering live 6.6 kV upstream busbar spouts and issue a signed High Voltage Permit-to-Work (PTW) or Sanction-for-Test (SFT).

Mission 01: Bow Thruster 6.6 kV Motor Isolation & PTW

Isolate the 1,800 kW Bow Thruster feeder (BT-M1): Equip PPE & air-test gloves → Trip VCB [O] → Rack to DISCONNECTED (0/20) → Extract K1-BT & padlock port → Insert K1-BT & close/padlock Earth Switch → Lock Key Box → Open 3D door, padlock Red Busbar Shutter, Prove–Test–Prove, & issue PTW.

Mission 02: Transformer Dual-End Isolation (The Back-Feed Trap)

Isolate HV/LV Transformer TR1 (6.6 kV / 440 V). Isolate & lock out 440 V ACB-TR1-LV FIRST to prevent lethal 440 V → 6.6 kV step-up back-feed, then isolate TR1-HV, insert both K-HV & K-LV into the dual-key Earth Switch, apply CME leads, and issue PTW.

Mission 03: Sanction-for-Test & 5 kV Megger / PI Test

Cancel active PTW → Issue Sanction-for-Test (SFT) → Open Earth Switch → Connect 5 kV Megger leads & run PI test (PI = 2.49 PASS) → Bleed 4,850 V DC capacitive charge to 0 V → Re-close Earth Switch & cancel SFT.

Mission 04: De-Isolation & Safe Restoration to Service

Cancel PTW → Remove CME phase clamps (L1, L2, L3) FIRST and PE Earth Bar clamp LAST → Unlock red shutter & close door → Open Earth Switch & return K1-BT to VCB → Rack to TEST (10/20) for Close/Trip check → Rack fully to SERVICE (20/20).

Q: How do I inspect internal vacuum interrupters and shutters in 3D?
Toggle X-Ray Glass in the top 3D bar, drag the Explode (0–100%) slider to separate all 7 subsystems along CAD guide axes, or click any Callout / Isolate chip (VI, SS, ES) for 14% native-color ghost isolation.
Q: Why is the racking crank blocked from turning?
Under IEC 60092-503 mechanical interlocks, the racking port flap is blocked whenever the VCB is CLOSED [I] or padlocked. Press TRIP [O] first!
Q: Why is Castell Key K1-BT trapped in the breaker?
Castell keys remain mechanically trapped at SERVICE (20) and TEST (10). Crank the VCB truck to DISCONNECTED (0/20) to release and extract the key.
Q: What is the Reverse CME Removal Rule in Mission 4?
Always unclamp phase leads (L1, L2, L3) FIRST via insulating hot-stick, and unclamp the Ship Hull Earth Bar (PE) LAST so the cable never floats at lethal induced potential.

📘 IEC 60092-503 Mechanical Interlocks & COSWP Chapter 22

The 5 Golden Rules of Marine HV Safety:
  1. Disconnect completely (All-pole isolation from all sources, including 440 V back-feed).
  2. Secure against re-connection (LOTO padlocks, shutter locks, Castell trapped keys).
  3. Verify dead (Prove – Test – Prove using rated 6.6 kV wand & proving unit).
  4. Carry out earthing and short-circuiting (Integral earth switch and/or portable CME leads).
  5. Provide protection against adjacent live parts & issue formal Permit-to-Work (PTW) or SFT.
