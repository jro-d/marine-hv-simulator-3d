Step-by-step tutorial

MCA COSWP Ch. 22 & STCW A-III/6. Work on equipment above 1,000 V follows the five golden rules, in this order.
1. Disconnect completely

Trip the VCB and rack the truck to DISCONNECTED 0/20. Isolate every source, including 440 V secondary back-feed.

2. Secure against re-connection

Padlock the racking port, extract the Castell key, transfer it to the earth switch, lock keys in the safety key box and post danger tags.

3. Verify dead — prove, test, prove

Prove the 6.6 kV wand on the proving unit, test L1, L2, L3, then prove the wand again.

4. Earth and short-circuit

Close the integral earth switch and/or apply CME leads — PE first, then phases.

5. Guard adjacent live parts & issue the permit

Padlock the red busbar shutter and issue a signed Permit-to-Work or Sanction-for-Test.

01 · Bow Thruster motor isolation & PTW

PPE → trip → rack out → extract K1-BT & padlock → close/padlock earth switch → lock key box → open door, padlock red shutter, prove–test–prove → issue PTW.

02 · Transformer dual-end isolation

Isolate the 440 V ACB-TR1-LV first (back-feed trap), then TR1-HV. Insert both K-HV and K-LV to close the earth switch, apply CME, issue PTW.

03 · Sanction-for-Test & 5 kV PI test

Cancel PTW → issue SFT → open earth switch → run test (PI 2.49) → discharge 4,850 V DC to 0 V → re-earth → cancel SFT.

04 · De-isolation & restoration

Cancel PTW → remove CME phases first, PE last → close door → open earth switch, return K1-BT → function test at TEST 10/20 → rack to SERVICE 20/20.

How do I look inside the switchgear?
Use X-ray to see through the steel panels and epoxy poles, drag Explode to separate the active bay into 19 sub-assemblies (they never touch at 100%), or click a part chip or label to isolate it.
Why is the racking crank blocked?
The racking port is interlocked while the VCB is closed or padlocked. Trip first.
Why is the Castell key trapped?
Keys release only at DISCONNECTED 0/20.
What is the reverse CME rule?
Remove phase clamps first and the PE clamp last, so the lead is never floating at induced voltage in your hands.
